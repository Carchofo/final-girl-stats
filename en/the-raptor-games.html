Small sample

Only 57 recorded games. Split across Dark Powers, Finales and Setups, each breakdown ends up with very few: take them as a hint, not a fact.

What makes it different

The pack's health is the number of raptors, with no cap. Loose raptors stop on victims instead of attacking them. The T-Rex cannot be killed, moves 2 per round and eats in a fixed order: victims, raptors, Final Girl, killer. Three electric fences block movement and line of sight; a die roll in Upkeep decides which are active. Rescuing costs 1 extra time. Preliminary data: the box is very recent and there is little experience with it.

How to start the game

The four openings, with which one to use depending on the setup, are in the opening strategies.

📖 Raptors guide (killer)

Works with any location. ↑ top

⌛ = Time · ✴ = damage · 🩸 = Bloodlust · 💔 = lose health · ❤️ = recover health · 🔧 = space with the orange wrench icon

Watch out

  • The Pack counts as the Killer for all purposes; individual Raptors are Minions. A card that says "Raptor(s)" doesn't include the Pack; one that says "Pack" doesn't include individual Raptors.
  • You win when the Pack is eliminated, even if individual Raptors are still on the board.
  • A Raptor that spawns during the Killer phase doesn't activate until the next Killer phase.
  • Individual Raptors stop when they enter (or start moving in) a space with you or a Victim. Location Terror cards don't show that icon, but the rule still applies.

Special setup

  • Shuffle the Spawn cards facedown next to the Raptors card, and put the Raptor meeples on that card.
  • The Pack starts on the Killer space (K) of the Setup card, instead of the core box Killer meeple.

The Pack

  • A large group of Raptors that counts as the Killer for all purposes.
  • Its health is the number of Raptors in it: it starts at 5 (1 Final Health and 4 Health) with no upper limit.
  • The Attack Value on the Killer board is what the Pack deals per attack, whatever its size.

Individual Raptors

  • The Raptor meeples. They are Minions for all purposes.
  • They have 1 health and use the Movement Value on the Killer board.
  • They don't attack often, but when they do, each one deals 1 ✴.

Joining the Pack

  • On the Pack icon, Raptors head for the Pack by the shortest path. If several are equal, they take the one that gets them closest to the Pack before stopping.
  • A Raptor in the Pack's space joins it immediately: remove the meeple and add 1 health to the Pack for each one.
  • Meeples removed from the board go back to the Raptors card for future spawns.

Spawning Raptors

  • On the spawn icon, draw a Spawn card and spawn that many Raptors. The space comes from a die roll on the Raptors card's table; if several are legal, you choose.
  • They never spawn in spaces that are no longer accessible.
  • If there aren't enough meeples, the Raptor closest to the Pack joins it and you use its meeple; repeat until the card is resolved.
  • If the Spawn deck runs out, shuffle the discards and draw.
  • The final effect on the 🩸 track also spawns a Raptor, like a Spawn card of 1.

The spawn table (Raptors card)

  • Roll a die to see where they spawn: 1 your space · 2 the green (exit) space closest to the Pack · 3 the 🔧 space closest to the Pack · 4 the green space furthest from the Pack · 5 the 🔧 space furthest from the Pack · 6 the space furthest from you.
  • Each loose Raptor: 1 ❤️, a 1 ✴ attack and movement equal to the Pack's. They don't move if they start with you or a Victim, and stop when entering a space with you or a Victim.

Reading their cards

  • The red silhouette of several Raptors = the Pack icon: loose Raptors head towards it to join. The yellow figures = target you or the closest Victim. The boot = move. The knife = attack (once per knife). The stop sign with figures = stops on reaching you or a Victim. The cracked egg = Raptors spawn.
  • "Pack only" is just the Pack; "Raptors only", just the loose ones.

Finales

  • All have two rows: on top, loose Raptors move towards the Pack and stop if they reach you or a Victim; below, the Pack targets you or the closest Victim, moves and attacks.
  • Claws Like Knives: the Pack attacks once. The first damage from each attack is the crossed-out heart icon.
  • Born Hungry: the Pack attacks twice. When revealed, draw a Spawn card but spawn the Raptors in your space.
  • Raptor Rush: the Pack attacks 3 times. When revealed, all Raptors on the board join the Pack.
  • Deadly Quick (Epic Finale): the Pack attacks twice. When revealed, place the Pack in your space and lose 4 ⌛.

Dark Powers

  • Pack Conditioning: any time a Raptor is adjacent to the Pack, add it to the Pack.
  • They're Everywhere!: when drawing Spawn cards, draw 2 and spawn the higher value. Then discard both.
  • Blood Thirsty: each time the Pack kills a Victim, it gains ❤️.
  • Scaly Hide (Epic Dark Power): the Pack and Raptors ignore all damage from Reaction cards.

Terror cards that need explaining

  • Protect the Brood (2 copies): Raptors spawn (egg) and you place the Pack where they spawned. If the Spawn card was a 0, roll a die and place the Pack using the table on the Raptors card. Then the Pack targets you or a Victim, moves and attacks.
  • Share the Feast: the Pack targets, moves and attacks; loose Raptors move towards the Pack (stopping at you or a Victim). If any joined the Pack, the Pack attacks again.
  • No Escape: if there are no Raptors on the board, discard it and draw the next Terror card. Otherwise, the nearest Raptor to each green (exit) space is placed there, and those on exits attack. If no one was attacked, 🎭🎭.
  • Predatory Instincts (Minor Dark Power): the Pack deals 1 extra ✴ per knife.
  • Survival Instincts (Minor Dark Power): each Upkeep the Pack gains ❤️.

📖 Isla Lagarto guide (location)

Works with any killer. ↑ top

Watch out

  • Lava spaces are no longer accessible to anyone, Raptors and T-Rex included.
  • If Game Makers is drawn during setup, discard it and draw another.

Special setup

  • Place Electric Fence markers in the green sections shown on the Setup card. Nothing goes in the red ones.
  • Shuffle the 3 Lava cards facedown and put the Lava tokens next to them.
  • Place the T-Rex card near the play area and its meeple on the card's start space.

Electric Fences

  • There are 3 fences: A, B and C. While a marker is there, the path is blocked and the spaces on each side are not adjacent.
  • Panic: anyone panicking towards a blocked fence stays where they are.
  • Farthest / closest: only count unblocked paths. If all 3 markers are out and the only space or target is on the other side, count as if there were no fences.
  • Enemy targeting: with no unblocked path to its target, the Enemy doesn't move for that action, but its attack icons still resolve in its space.
  • Placing someone: fences don't affect effects that place someone.
  • Each Upkeep, roll the fence die: if that fence has a marker, remove it; if not, place one.

The T-Rex

  • It starts locked in the Dino Pen. With its icon (the footprint), move its meeple one space right on the T-Rex card.
  • On "Break out!", put its meeple in the Dino Pen on the board and its action token below the Killer Action on the Finale card. From then on, ignore its icon.
  • It is not a Killer or a Minion: it can't be attacked, damaged or killed. Its action resolves after the Finale's Minion and Killer Actions.
  • Victims won't follow you into its space (unless an effect lets them follow you into the Killer's). In the Panic phase, everyone in its space panics, whether or not anyone died that turn.
  • Movement 2, with the ANY icon: it stops as soon as it reaches a space with anyone (you, Victims, Enemies...), and won't move if someone is already in its space.
  • It doesn't deal damage: it kills one target in its space. Priority: Victim, Minion, you, Killer. If it attacks you or the Killer, the target dies instantly, without revealing Final Health.
  • Against an Enemy made of several units (the Raptor Pack, a zombie horde...) it kills one unit, i.e. 1 damage.

The volcano

  • The first time you resolve the volcano icon, it erupts: everyone at the Volcano takes 💔, anyone left in the space panics, and you place the Volcano Lava token there: it's no longer accessible.
  • After that, the lava spreads to an adjacent space: draw a Lava card to see which one and follow it.
  • Once all 4 Lava tokens are on the board, ignore the volcano icon.
  • The 3 Lava cards: North Beach, Generator and Smuggler's Cove. Everyone there takes 💔, anyone left panics until they leave the space, and you place its Lava token.

Special spaces

  • The 3 corner spaces with the beach icon are Beach spaces.
  • The 2 edge spaces with the cliff icon are Cliff spaces.
  • Several location cards refer to them.

Tropical Storm

Herbal Remedies
Take a killed Victim of your choice and place it in your space. Discard.
The Hunter
Place the Hunter in the space furthest from you. Each Upkeep, the Hunter panics and deals ✴ to everyone in his space.
