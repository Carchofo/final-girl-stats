Mort on Mega BGCon. Across 122 recorded games it is won 70.5% of the time, 5.0 points above the game average (65.5%).

What makes it different

Mort does not start on the board: he is hidden among 10 randomly placed suspects. Interrogate and Coerce cards reveal clues that eliminate suspects until one is left. Until he is unmasked, certain Terror cards let a random suspect act as the killer. Victims don't panic until he is unmasked. A tongue-in-cheek homage to Scream. It is the most recent release, and there is little experience with it yet.

How Mort works

  • Base movement: moves in its Killer Action before attacking.
  • Starts as one of 10 Suspects (Killers from other Feature Films); you must Interrogate and Coerce five, turning them into Informants.
  • When his disguise is revealed or the Terror deck runs out, he takes his final form.
  • Before the Finale: draw a Suspect token for the Active Killer > Target Victim or Final Girl > Move > Attack.
