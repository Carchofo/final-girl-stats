Razorface on Hellscape. Across 127 recorded games it is won 62.2% of the time, 3.3 points below the game average (65.5%).

What makes it different

Afflictions work as optional objectives on top of the normal win condition. A community favourite as a Series 3 starting point: demanding without being overwhelming.

LevelMedium+
The mapModular tiles, the best way into Series 3

How Razorface works

  • You start with 3 Affliction cards; to kill him you must Purge each by meeting its Purge Requirement in the Action phase.
  • An impossible Affliction is removed and replaced; you lose if you must draw one and none remain.
  • His Final Health token can only be revealed with no Afflictions in play; such damage is ignored otherwise.

The map: Hellscape

17 spaces (9 on the main board and 8 on the shifting Hellscape tiles). 3 exits: southwest, northeast and the central Nexus, which is also a Search space alongside the House of Mutilation and the Sepulchre of Blood.

  • Four double-sided Hellscape tiles sit around the outer perimeter of the board.
  • Each Upkeep phase special dice are rolled: either a tile flips or two tiles swap places.
  • Other effects can change the tiles, and the player can move them with certain Items or the unique Action card Hellshift.
  • Hellshift: 2 successes rearrange all tiles; 1 success swaps one tile and -1 Time; 0 successes -2 Time.

Unique cards: Hellscape tiles, Hellshift
