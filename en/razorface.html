Across 511 recorded games, the Final Girl beats Razorface 53.8% of the time. The average for the whole game is 65.5%, so that is below average: one of the tough ones.

Who is Razorface

A demonic humanoid with no lips or nose, his body covered in ritual cuts and hooks chained into his skin.

How Razorface plays

9 health and Horror 5. You start with 3 Affliction cards on him, and to really kill him you have to purge each one by meeting its requirement.

Comes in the Hell to Pay box, paired with Hellscape.

The map changes the fight

On Hellscape, its box location, the Final Girl wins 62.2%, against 53.8% across all maps: +8.4 points. That location suits the killer: don't trust the average. The best map is Storybook Woods (71.0%) and the worst is Sacred Groves (27.1%).
