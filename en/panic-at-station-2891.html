The Organism on Station 2891. Across 164 recorded games it is won 65.2% of the time, right on the game average (65.5%).

What makes it different

Victims get tested for infection. Infected ones turn into independent killers. It is accused of heavy upkeep; others say the two extra systems run smoothly once understood.

LevelAdvanced
The mapHelicopter rescue instead of exits, freezing mechanic

How The Organism works

  • Three Victims are randomly replaced with Exposed Victims that may be Organisms.
  • A Test Kit, the Lab space or their death draws a Test Result card: SAFE makes a normal Victim; ASSIMILATED makes an Organism.
  • Mutated Organisms are Killers, not Minions, acting one at a time from the leftmost on the Killer board.
  • In the Finale each Organism gets extra Killer actions at the start of the Killer phase.

The map: Station 2891

20 spaces and one exit, the helipad to the south. Search spaces: Commander's Hut, Supply Closet and Tool Shed; other named spaces: Kitchen, Lab, Radio Room and Rec Room. Two spaces are hallways and five are outside spaces, including the helipad.

  • With the Helicopter on the helipad you can place up to three Victims in it, so they cannot be targeted or killed.
  • Those Victims are not saved until the Helicopter reaches the Arctic Airport space on its separate card.
  • The Helicopter moves via the Fly Faster Action card or the Helicopter icon on several Terror cards.
  • Frostbite: you, each Victim and each Enemy in an outdoor space gain a Frostbite token, or lose 1 Health if already frostbitten.
  • Each Upkeep phase, discard all Frostbite tokens for anyone indoors.

Unique cards: Helicopter card, Fly Faster

Tips from players

Forum strategy: test the two exposed victims closest to the lab as soon as you can and let the third mutate on its own. Keeping one organism alive at low health gives you the adrenaline die and avoids the "if there is no organism" effects. The axe is ideal because they have low health. And you can send victims to the helicopter by radio without being there yourself.
