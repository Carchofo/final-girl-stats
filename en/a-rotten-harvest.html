Grimlash on Shady Acres. Across 87 recorded games it is won 67.8% of the time, 2.5 points above the game average (65.3%).

Small sample

What makes it different

Harvest Madness track: a heart either heals OR lowers madness, never both. Levels 1-3 give a bonus of your choice. Going past level 3 is an instant loss. The children kidnap victims to take them to the altar. The risk-reward tension of that track is highly rated.

How Grimlash works

  • Base movement: moves in its Killer Action before attacking.
  • Starts at 4 Horror and 10 Health.
  • Inflicts Harvest Madness, which grants the Final Girl extra abilities but eventually causes death.
  • Initial Killer phase: Target Final Girl or Victim > Move 1 > Attack > if a Victim was killed, 1 Harvest Madness.

The map: Shady Acres

18 spaces and 2 exits (southeast and northwest). Search spaces: Old Barn, McCoy Farmhouse and Ralph's Garage. Six spaces are Field Spaces (wheat icon) and one in the northeast is the Altar of the Old Gods.

  • The Children are Enemies (not Minions) and act in the Killer phase before the Minion or Killer, using their action token: Target Final Girl or Victim > Move > Abduct.
  • On a 1 or 2, Abduct removes a Victim and a Child to the Altar of the Old Gods and sacrifices the Victim (increasing Bloodlust as usual).
  • Each sacrificed Victim goes on top of the Ancient Curses deck; when three Victims have been sacrificed, a card is drawn from it.
  • One Child spawns each Upkeep phase, unless all three Children or zero Victims are already on the board.

Unique cards: Ancient Curses (8), Children

How to start the game

The four openings, with which one to use depending on the setup, are in the opening strategies.

📖 Grimlash guide (killer)

Works with any location. ↑ top

🩸 = Bloodlust · 🌾 = Field Space · 🎭 = raise Horror · ⊘🎭 = lower Horror

Common mistakes

  • Grimlash moves 1 in his Killer Action. When a Terror card shows the boot (move) icon, he moves whatever his movement is at the current 🩸 level, not 1.
  • A ❤️ heal does not heal and lower Harvest Madness at once: each heart does one or the other.
  • When Harvest Madness drops a level, you lose that level's card.

Harvest Madness 🎃 · setup

  • Split the Harvest Madness cards by level and shuffle each: 3 decks.
  • Put the marker on the starting space of the Harvest Madness track (outlined in orange), near the top of the Killer board.

Harvest Madness 🎃 · how it works

  • Each time you see the 🎃 icon, move the marker 1 space right.
  • When it reaches a new level, draw 2 cards from that level's deck, keep 1 and discard the other. Its effect lasts while you stay at that level or above.
  • If you go beyond level 3 (the skull space), you lose the game immediately.
  • When resolving a ❤️ heal, for each heart you choose: recover health as normal or lower Harvest Madness by 1. Never both.
  • If you drop to a lower level, discard the card for the level you left. If you later return to it, draw 2 again and keep 1. If the deck runs short, reshuffle that level's discards.
  • Once-per-turn effects are used by flipping the card facedown; it flips back faceup in the Upkeep.

Finales

  • Base Finale: targets you or the closest Victim, moves 1 space and attacks. If a Victim was killed, 🎃 (Harvest Madness goes up).
  • Hunger of the Old Gods: targets, moves 2 and attacks twice. At the end of the Killer phase, all Victims move 1 space towards Grimlash.
  • Withered Harbinger: targets, moves and attacks twice. For every space Grimlash moves during the Killer phase, lose ⌛.
  • Ancient Abomination: targets, moves 2 and attacks. Whenever he resolves an attack, add the Harvest Madness level to his Attack Value.
  • God of Blood and Grain (Epic Finale): targets you, moves and attacks twice. When revealed, kill all Victims.

Dark Powers

  • Root-Bound Curse: whenever you resolve ❤️ to heal or reduce Harvest Madness, Grimlash recovers half that much ❤️, rounded up.
  • Rite of the Corrupted: during Upkeep, 🎃 if Harvest Madness did not increase this turn.
  • Rite of the Old One: whenever Harvest Madness increases to a new level, discard the next Terror card.
  • Fields of Madness (Epic Dark Power): your Horror Rolls change with the Harvest Madness level. Level 0 or 1: one die fewer (minimum 1). Level 2: no effect. Level 3: one extra die.
  • Burning Sickle (Minor Dark Power): whenever Grimlash deals you damage, also deal ✴ to every Victim in your space.

Terror cards

  • "Something's Wrong with the Kids": the Children treat all 🌾 spaces as adjacent to each other. Then, Children only: target you or the closest Victim, move and resolve the hands icon (abduction). Then 🎭.
  • "Nobody Knows Where They Take Them...": if there are no Victims or Children on the board, discard and draw the next Terror card. Otherwise, panic all Victims in the same space as a Child. Then, for each Child with a Victim in their space, resolve the hands icon as if you had rolled a 1.

📖 Shady Acres guide (location)

Works with any killer. ↑ top

Common mistakes

  • The Children are not Minions, but they are Enemies: they don't resolve Killer or Minion actions on Terror cards unless the card says the Children do it.
  • If you mix Shady Acres with another killer, the Children still aren't Minions: that killer's cards that mention Minions don't affect them.
  • If you attack the Children and there are several in the space, you attack all of them and split the damage; leftover damage is lost.
  • An abduction in a space with no Victims isn't wasted: it counts as an attack.

Special setup

  • Shuffle the Ancient Curse cards into a facedown deck.
  • Put the Children card near the board, one Child on the Altar of the Old Gods and the rest on the card.
  • Place the Children Action token above the Minion or Killer actions on the Finale card: they act before any other Enemies.

The Children

  • Their card shows their health, attack and movement. They are Enemies, not Minions.
  • They act at the start of the Killer phase, before Minions and Killer, one at a time: closest to the Altar first.
  • Each Upkeep a Child spawns on the Altar, unless all three are already out or there are no Victims left.
  • They can be attacked and killed: a dead Child goes back to the card and can spawn again.
  • Victims follow you into a space with Children, unless the Killer is there too.
  • In the Panic phase, Victims in a space with a Child panic if at least one Victim was killed that turn.

Abductions

  • When a Child resolves the abduction icon in a space with a Victim, roll a die.
  • 1-2: the Victim is sacrificed on the Altar (killed). The Child goes back to the Altar, 🩸 goes up as usual and the Victim goes on the back of the top Ancient Curse card.
  • 3-6: nothing happens.

Ancient Curses

  • With 3 sacrificed Victims on it, remove them and reveal the curse, which comes into play. Several can be active at once.

Field Spaces 🌾

  • Spaces with the wheat icon are Field Spaces. Lots of cards in this box use them, for better and worse.
