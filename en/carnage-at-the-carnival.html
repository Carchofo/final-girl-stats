Geppetto on Carnival of Blood. Across 233 recorded games it is won 44.2% of the time, 21.3 points below the game average (65.5%).

What makes it different

3 puppets as minions. Immortal Master rule: you cannot kill Geppetto while all 3 are in play. Traps on items and on Terror cards. It can feel too easy if you clear the puppets early.

LevelAdvanced
The mapGreat theme, the traps feel passive

How Geppetto works

  • Deploys up to 3 Puppets (Minions) from the Puppets card; before the Finale he spawns 1 Puppet each Killer phase.
  • Puppets have 1 Health, deal 1 Damage, share Geppetto's Movement and must end within 2 spaces of him.
  • Terror card actions not limited to Puppets are resolved by each Puppet, then by Geppetto.

The map: Carnival of Blood

17 spaces and 3 exits (entrance and exit gates in the top-left and bottom-right corners, and the Clown Car near the center). Search spaces: Forest of Horrors, House of Mirrors and Things to Astonish; other named spaces: Animal Cages and The Big Top.

  • Item Traps are shuffled into the Item deck.
  • Terror Traps can be drawn during the Killer phase.
  • Each trap has different detrimental effects and can affect the Final Girl and any Victims in the space.

Unique cards: Item Traps (3), Terror Traps (3)
