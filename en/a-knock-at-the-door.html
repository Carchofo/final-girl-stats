📖 The Intruders guide (killer) · Wingard Cottage guide (location)

The Intruders on Wingard Cottage. Across 213 recorded games it is won 60.6% of the time, 4.7 points below the game average (65.3%).

What makes it different

3 killers at once, one of them always the "active" one, and it keeps changing. Its own weapon crafting system. Despite the three killers, it is surprisingly easy to follow. It never overwhelms.

LevelAdvanced
The mapThe best multi-killer format, with crafting

How The Intruders works

  • A trio of killers with 6 health each; they start at Horror 3 in the same space.
  • The Killer Action on their sheet is for "All Killers": each one, from the top down, attacks whoever is in its space. They do not move as part of it.
  • Only the Active Killer resolves Terror Cards.
  • The Active Killer changes when another one is attacked or damaged, through card effects, or when the active one dies.

The map: Wingard Cottage

20 spaces (10 indoor, 10 outdoor) and 3 exits (northeast, southwest and southeast, the latter labelled the Driveway). Search spaces: Boathouse, Garage and Shed; other named spaces: Bathroom, Family Room, Foyer, Kitchen, Lakefront, Laundry Room, Porch and two Bedrooms. The Lake to the northwest is labelled but not visitable.

  • In four spaces you can spend Time to pick up their Supply Item: Discarded Tools (Garage), Nails (Shed), Rope (Boathouse) and Wood (space north of the Garage).
  • Combining the right Supply Items with the Item and Time a recipe requires creates that Crafted Item.
  • The used Items and Supply Items are discarded.

Unique cards: Supply Items (4), Crafted Items

How to start the game

📖 The Intruders guide (killer)

Works with any location. ↑ top · The Intruders page → · Wingard Cottage guide (location)

⌛ = Time · ✴ = damage · ❤️ = recover health · 💔 = lose health · 🎭 = raise Horror · ⊘🎭 = lower Horror · 🎲 = die · 🩸 = Bloodlust · = Active Killer token moves UP to the next Killer · = moves DOWN to the next Killer

Common mistakes

  • If you attack a Killer, it becomes Active even if you deal no damage. Same if you use a card to damage it.
  • In the Panic phase, Victims in a space with any Killer, not just the Active one, panic if a Victim was killed that turn.
  • With a Minor Dark Power in play, damage to any Killer goes to that card first.
  • Each Killer has its own Final Health token, but you only get the +1 🎲 Final Health bonus once.
  • If a Killer that still has its black Final Health token dies while another is still on the board, the phase does not end there: knock the figure over and carry on.

Special setup

  • Place the three Killer figures (red from the Core Box, black and grey) on the Killer start space of the Setup card.
  • Each Killer gets its starting health, including one Final Health token each.
  • Put the Active Killer token on the Killer board, on Trish's circle (the red one).
  • According to the wiki, they start at 3 Horror and 6 Health each.

The Active Killer

  • The Intruders are 3 Killers: Trish, Baghead and Zeke. Only one is active when resolving move (boot), attack (knife) or placing the Killer.
  • The Active Killer is the one with the Active Killer token on its circle on the Killer board. The other two stay put.

How the Active Killer changes

  • It can change several times in the same turn. There are 3 ways:
  • 1. When you attack a Killer (whether or not you damage it) or use a card to damage it, it immediately becomes the Active Killer.
  • 2. With the icon (K with the red half on top) the token moves UP to the next Killer; with (red half at the bottom), DOWN. If it's on the top-most Killer and must go up, it wraps around to the bottom one, and vice versa.
  • 3. When the Active Killer dies, the token moves to the top-most living Killer.
  • Dead Killers are skipped when finding the next one.

Rulebook example: Ginny shoots Trish with the Sawed-Off Shotgun for 3 ✴; Trish becomes Active and has 1 Health left. In the Killer phase Trish attacks and Ginny finishes her with a Counterattack: Trish's figure is knocked over (without revealing her Final Health token yet) and the Active Killer token moves to the top-most living Killer, Baghead. The Terror card is "They're everywhere!": Baghead does the 1st action; the 2nd starts with and, as Trish is down, the token wraps around to Zeke; the 3rd, again, goes back to Baghead. When the phase ends, Trish's Final Health token is revealed: it's blank, so Trish leaves the board. If it had health, she would have stood back up.

All Killers, panic and Minor Dark Powers

  • "All Killers": ignore the token and resolve the effect for each one, starting with the top-most on the Killer board and going down. The Active Killer token doesn't move.
  • Panic: Victims in a space with any Killer panic in the Panic phase if a Victim was killed that turn.
  • Minor Dark Powers: they apply to all Killers. When any Killer is damaged, the damage goes to the Minor Dark Power card first.

Death of an Intruder and Final Health

  • If a Killer with its black Final Health token loses its last health while another Killer is still on the board, knock the figure over, change the Active Killer and finish the current phase.
  • When the phase ends, reveal its Final Health token. Blank: it's really dead and leaves the board. With health: restore its health as usual, swap the black token for a white one and stand it back up.
  • Even with 3 Final Health tokens, you only get the +1 🎲 bonus once.

Finales (from the wiki)

  • Hunt Sweet Hunt: the Active Killer targets you or a Victim, moves twice, attacks and . Each attack does 1 extra ✴.
  • You're Gonna Die: the Active Killer targets, moves and attacks; ; and the new Active Killer targets, moves and attacks.
  • Release the Beast: all Killers target, move and attack. From then on all Killers count as Active for the rest of the game; resolve their actions top to bottom.
  • We Know Where You Live (Epic Finale): the Active Killer targets, moves, attacks twice and . When revealed, all Killers target, move and attack.

Dark Powers

  • No Remorse: roll a die at the start of each Upkeep. If the result is greater than the number of Killers, 🩸.
  • Strength in Numbers: each attack does 1 extra ✴ for each other Killer in the same or an adjacent space.
  • Maskaphobia: once per turn, when a Killer moves into your space, 🎭. You can't buy Focus while a Killer is in your space.
  • Won't Stay Down (Epic Dark Power): when a Killer dies, it immediately restores 2 normal health and a random Final Health token. Then remove this card from the game.

Terror cards worth explaining (from the wiki)

  • "They will hunt you" (x2): place the Active Killer in your space and it attacks you. Then and the new one targets you and moves; again and the same.
  • "Where are they coming from?": the Active Killer targets, moves twice, attacks and . If there are no Victims, discard and draw another. Otherwise place the Active Killer with the furthest Victim and it attacks.
  • "Go away!!!": each Killer in your space or a space with a Victim takes 1 ✴. Each Killer damaged this way targets you or a Victim and attacks. Then draw an Event.
  • "They're everywhere!": three actions in a row, with before the 2nd and the 3rd. It's the one in the rulebook example.
  • Amphetamines (Minor Dark Power, 2 bonus health): at the start of the Killer phase, the Killer with the lowest health recovers 2.
  • Coordinated Attack (Minor Dark Power, 2 bonus health): at the end of each Killer phase, and the new Active Killer targets you or a Victim, moves and attacks.

📖 Wingard Cottage guide (location)

Works with any killer. ↑ top · The Intruders guide (killer)

Common mistakes

  • You can only pick up a Supply Item if you are in its space and pay ⌛: passing through doesn't count.
  • Discarded Supply Items and Crafted Items go back faceup to the play area, ready to be picked up or crafted again.
  • If you craft using a limited-use Item, the Crafted Item keeps its remaining uses (Shotgun → Sawed-Off Shotgun, Trashcan Lid → Porcupine).
  • House and Indoors aren't the same: the Garage is House; the Shed and the Boathouse are Indoors but not House.

Special setup

  • Place the four Supply Item cards faceup in the play area.
  • Shuffle the Crafted Item cards and reveal the top four faceup in the play area.

House, Indoors and Outdoors

  • House: Kitchen, both Bedrooms, Bathroom, Family Room, Foyer, Laundry Room and Garage.
  • Indoors: every space in the house plus the Shed and the Boathouse.
  • Outdoors: every space that isn't Indoors.

Supply Items

  • There are 4: Discarded Tools in the Garage, Nails in the Shed, Rope in the Boathouse and Wood in the spaces with the wood icon.
  • When you're in a space with its icon, you may take the card by paying ⌛. You can't do it while just passing through.
  • It goes into a Backpack slot. They're only used for crafting.
  • When discarded, it goes back faceup to the play area and can be taken again.

Crafted Items

  • Crafting: take an available Crafted Item by discarding the Items and Supply Items listed on its card and paying the ⌛ shown.
  • The Crafted Item goes to your hand or a Backpack slot. When discarded, it goes back faceup to the play area and can be crafted again.
  • If you discard a limited-use Item to craft, the new one keeps those uses: a Shotgun with 1 use left gives a Sawed-Off Shotgun with 1 use. Same with the Trashcan Lid and the Porcupine.
  • Only 4 are available at the start. Blueprint and the Brainstorming event add more.

Rulebook example: Ava has a Wooden Bat and Nails. She spends 2 ⌛, crafts the Nail Bat and puts it in her hand. She discards the Wooden Bat and returns the Nails faceup next to the other Supply Items.

Location Terror cards (from the wiki)

  • "Now get the hell out of my house!": if you're not in the house, discard and draw another. If you are, draw an Event and place the Killer in your space. If you have Weak Attack in hand, you may play it; otherwise it attacks you.
  • "Is Tammy home?": place the Killer on the Porch. 🎭.
  • "They can't get in here. Right?": 2 new Victims in the Bedroom. 🎭.
  • "What happened while we were gone?": 3 new Victims at the Boathouse. Draw an Event.
  • "We were passing by and thought we heard a scream.": 2 new Victims on the Porch, 🎭 and draw an Event.
  • With the Intruders, "the Killer" on these cards is the Active Killer.
