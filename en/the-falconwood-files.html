Slayer on Falconwood. Across 126 recorded games it is won 70.6% of the time, 5.1 points above the game average (65.5%).

What makes it different

There is a hidden mirror dimension, a parallel version of every space. A mission system with rewards to choose from. Highly rated for replayability: the choice of missions makes every game play out differently.

LevelAdvanced
The mapMissions and a mirror dimension, lots of replayability

The map: Falconwood

24 spaces and 3 exits (north, south and southeast). Search spaces: Government Lab, Police Station and Shopping Mall; other named spaces: Best Friend's House, Community Pool, Fanghorn Forest, Library, School and Town Fair.

  • River Crossings: spaces linked by blue lines are not adjacent and cannot normally be crossed unless a special event allows it.
  • At the start you choose a Mission; completing it is mandatory to win.
  • You cannot turn over the Killer's final health marker until the Mission is completed.
  • Completing the Mission grants a Friend: a Special Victim who will not panic and enters the Killer's space with you.
