Ratchet Lady on Wolfe Asylum. Across 148 recorded games it is won 50.0% of the time, 15.5 points below the game average (65.5%).

What makes it different

She heals with no cap as long as you don't attack her. Amped cards (upgraded actions) through the pill system. Described as unpredictable: it depends hugely on how early you take her on.

LevelAdvanced
The mapPill system and Amped cards, "Final Girl 2.0" card

How Ratchet Lady works

  • Base movement: does not move in its Killer Action; attacks whoever is in its space.
  • Accompanied by Minions called Maniacs; you have access to Calm Action cards.
  • Before the Finale: Minions: Target Final Girl > Move; Killer: Target Final Girl or Victim > Attack.

The map: Wolfe Asylum

20 spaces and 3 exits (top left, bottom center and right middle). Search spaces: Doctor's Office, Utility Closet and Medical Storage; other named locations: Operating Theater, Common Room and Solitary Confinement.

  • Includes Pills and Stunned gameplay.
  • Picking up a Pill costs 1 Time after ending movement in its space; its color is revealed on pickup.
  • One Pill: Yellow 1 Health, Purple move 1 space, Pink minus 1 Horror; no Side Effects cards.
  • Two Pills give stronger effects and draw 2 Side Effects; all three (Yellow + Purple + Pink) draw an Amped card and 3 Side Effects.

Unique cards: Amped (5), Side Effects (10), Pills

Tips from players

She doesn't chase you herself; she sends Maniacs who come only for you, relentlessly. And careful: Maniacs cause direct health loss, which can NOT be blocked with Guard or Retaliate. Defensive cards do little here. Go for mobility, rescue fast and always keep an attack in hand.

What reviews say

Highly rated for design, and one of the hardest in the game. Three reviews agree: the asylum and the pills are loved, Ratchet Lady frustrates. Not a box to start with.
