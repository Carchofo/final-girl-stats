📖 Ratchet Lady guide (killer) · Wolfe Asylum guide (location)

Ratchet Lady on Wolfe Asylum. Across 150 recorded games it is won 50.0% of the time, 15.3 points below the game average (65.3%).

What makes it different

She heals with no cap as long as you don't attack her. Amped cards (upgraded actions) through the pill system. Described as unpredictable: it depends hugely on how early you take her on.

LevelAdvanced
The mapPill system and Amped cards, "Final Girl 2.0" card

How Ratchet Lady works

  • Base movement: does not move in its Killer Action; attacks whoever is in its space.
  • Accompanied by Minions called Maniacs; you have access to Calm Action cards.
  • Before the Finale: Minions: Target Final Girl > Move; Killer: Target Final Girl or Victim > Attack.

The map: Wolfe Asylum

20 spaces and 3 exits (top left, bottom center and right middle). Search spaces: Doctor's Office, Utility Closet and Medical Storage; other named locations: Operating Theater, Common Room and Solitary Confinement.

  • Includes Pills and Stunned gameplay.
  • Picking up a Pill costs 1 Time after ending movement in its space; its color is revealed on pickup.
  • One Pill: Yellow 1 Health, Purple move 1 space, Pink minus 1 Horror; no Side Effects cards.
  • Two Pills give stronger effects and draw 2 Side Effects; all three (Yellow + Purple + Pink) draw an Amped card and 3 Side Effects.

Unique cards: Amped (5), Side Effects (10), Pills

Tips from players

She doesn't chase you herself; she sends Maniacs who come only for you, relentlessly. And careful: Maniacs cause direct health loss, which can NOT be blocked with Guard or Retaliate. Defensive cards do little here. Go for mobility, rescue fast and always keep an attack in hand.

What reviews say

Highly rated for design, and one of the hardest in the game. Three reviews agree: the asylum and the pills are loved, Ratchet Lady frustrates. Not a box to start with.

How to start the game

📖 Ratchet Lady guide (killer)

Works with any location. ↑ top

Card names from the Spanish edition · ⌛ = Time · ✴ = damage · ❤️ = heal · 💔 = lose health · 🎭 = Horror goes up · 🩸 = Bloodlust · 💊 = Pastilla (pill) token

Watch out

  • Ratchet Lady starts with only 6 health but has no cap: she heals in many ways.
  • Leaving a space with Lunáticos (Lunatics) costs 💔 for each Lunático beyond the first.
  • Turning a Victim into a Lunático does not raise 🩸, and neither does killing a Lunático.

Special setup

  • Make a pile of Lunático figures next to the Victim figures.
  • Place 2 Lunáticos in Ratchet Lady's starting space.

The Lunáticos

  • They are Minions: former Victims who have gone mad. They follow the Minion rules (p. 32 of the core rulebook).
  • They have 1 health, and Attack and Movement of 1. They don't follow you and can't be rescued.
  • When a Victim becomes a Lunático, swap its figure for a red Lunático figure. 🩸 doesn't go up: nobody died.
  • When a Lunático dies, its figure goes back to the supply. 🩸 doesn't go up either, even though it used to be a Victim.
  • If you want to leave a space with Lunáticos, you lose 💔 for each Lunático beyond the first: with one nothing happens, with three you lose 2.
  • Maximum 6 Lunáticos on the board at once. If you must place another and none are left, ignore it.
  • Special Victims do not become Lunáticos; only normal ones do.

Calmar

  • Against Ratchet Lady you get the Calmar (Calm) Action cards, which turn Lunáticos back into Victims.
  • When you successfully resolve a Calmar, swap the number of Lunáticos it shows for Victim figures and return the Lunáticos to the supply. They can now follow you, you can rescue them, and Enemies can kill them.

Finales

  • Llueven Lunáticos: top, she goes after you, moves and attacks 2 times. Bottom, 2 Lunáticos appear in her space, she goes after the Victim or you and attacks. If there are no Lunáticos when it's revealed, 2 appear in Ratchet Lady's space.
  • Alimentando la Locura: top, she goes, moves and attacks. Bottom, Ratchet Lady heals ❤️, then goes, moves and attacks. At the start of each Killer phase, if there are no Lunáticos, one appears in her space.
  • Sujetadla: top, she goes after you and moves. Bottom, she goes after you, moves and attacks 2 times. When it's revealed, all remaining Victims become Lunáticos and all Calmar Action cards are removed from the game.

Dark Powers

  • Inyección Oscura: in the Killer phase, the first time Ratchet Lady heals at least 1 ❤️, she heals another.
  • Brote Maníaco: when a Lunático enters the board, immediately trigger its Minion Action.
  • Miedo a la Sangre: in the Killer phase, the first time Horror or 🩸 goes up by at least 1, Ratchet Lady heals ❤️.
  • Escudo de Locura (Epic Dark Power): Ratchet Lady can't lose health while there are Lunáticos on the board.

📖 Wolfe Asylum guide (location)

Works with any killer. ↑ top

Watch out

  • You only pick up a 💊 if you end your movement in its space, not if you're passing through.
  • Taking 2 or 3 Pastillas at once forces you to draw Efectos Secundarios (side effects) cards.

Special setup

  • Make a pile of Aturdido (Stunned) tokens next to the play area.
  • Shuffle the Pastilla tokens face down (blank side up) and make a pile.
  • The game starts with 4 Pastilla tokens on the board: the Setup card says where. Draw them from the pile and place them face down.
  • Place the Efectos de las Pastillas card next to your player board. Draw 1 Pastilla token and place it face up below it. There should be 5 left in the supply.
  • Shuffle the Dopaje and Efectos Secundarios decks and put them next to the play area.

Pastillas, Efectos Secundarios and Dopaje

  • There are 4 kinds of Pastillas: 3 yellow, 3 blue, 3 pink and 1 wild (counts as any color).
  • If you end your movement in a space with a Pastilla token (not passing through), you can spend ⌛ to take it. Place it face up below your Efectos de las Pastillas card.
  • At any point in the Action phase you can take the ones you've collected, discarding their tokens. If the pile runs out, shuffle the discarded ones and make a new face-down pile.
  • Check the Efectos de las Pastillas card to see what each one does. If you take 2 or 3 at once, after applying their effects you draw 1 or more Efectos Secundarios cards, one at a time.
  • You can take as many as you like, in any order: for example a single one, then two together (drawing Efectos Secundarios for those two).
  • If you take one of each color, you can draw a Dopaje card: they give very powerful abilities.

Aturdido

  • Some items have the word Aturdido. Only Enemies can be Aturdido: place an Aturdido token under them.
  • In the next Killer phase, ignore all their movement (but still resolve their attacks).
  • In Upkeep, discard all Aturdido tokens.
