Hunter70.9% win rate

ES · EN · FR · DE · IT

Across 470 recorded games, the Final Girl beats Hunter 70.9% of the time. The average for the whole game is 65.5%, so that is above average: one of the easier killers.

Who is Hunter

An android hunter. There are actually two: the Mark V, tall and hooded, who disguises himself as a human, and the Mark X, more agile and better built.

How Hunter plays

15 health and Horror 5, but that health only counts for the Mark X. A counter swaps the Mark V for the Mark X near the end.

Game details from the Final Girl Wiki, under a CC BY-SA licence.

How Hunter works

  • Base movement: moves in its Killer Action before attacking.
  • Before the Finale: Target Final Girl > Movement > Attack.
  • First you face the Mark V, tracked by 5 Cybernetic Part tokens; each needs 2 damage in the same phase.
  • Bloodlust only rises when it damages the Final Girl or an ally, or is damaged, at most once per phase.
  • When all parts are gone the Mark V shuts down and its Dark Power goes under the Mark V Ally card; the Mark X follows.

Summarised from the Final Girl Wiki page (CC BY-SA).

Comes in the The Killer from Tomorrow box, paired with Sunnydaze Mall.

The map changes the fight

On Sunnydaze Mall, its box location, the Final Girl wins 60.7%, against 70.9% across all maps: -10.1 points. That location suits the killer: don't trust the average. The best map is USS Konrad (93.0%) and the worst is Marrek Warehouse (56.9%).
