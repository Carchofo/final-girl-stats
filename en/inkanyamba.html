Across 588 recorded games, the Final Girl beats Inkanyamba 63.8% of the time. The average for the whole game is 65.5%, so that is right on the average.

Who is Inkanyamba

A masked man who believes he is a holy avenger and has forgotten his own name. He fights with two daggers.

How Inkanyamba plays

9 health and Horror 4. He raises his own Killer Wrath every phase while it is low, so he gets more dangerous the longer the game lasts.

Game details from the Final Girl Wiki, under a CC BY-SA licence.

How Inkanyamba works

  • Base movement: does not move in its Killer Action; attacks whoever is in its space.
  • Uses the Killer Wrath card with a marker starting at level 2, ranging from 1 to 10.
  • Before the Finale: if Killer Wrath is 1-2, increase it by 1 > Target Final Girl or Victim > Attack.
  • Unleash Killer Wrath applies the current level's effects, such as raising Horror, a Killer action or extra Health.
  • Atonement Action cards, played in the Action phase, are the main way to reduce Wrath.

Summarised from the Final Girl Wiki page (CC BY-SA).

Comes in the Slaughter in the Groves box, paired with Sacred Groves.

The map changes the fight

On Sacred Groves, its box location, the Final Girl wins 38.3%, against 63.8% across all maps: -25.5 points. That location suits the killer: don't trust the average. The best map is USS Konrad (88.2%) and the worst is Sacred Groves (38.3%).
