The Intruders62.5% win rate

🇪🇸 ES · 🇬🇧 EN · 🇫🇷 FR · 🇩🇪 DE · 🇮🇹 IT

Across 936 recorded games, the Final Girl beats The Intruders 62.5% of the time. The average for the whole game is 65.4%, so that is right on the average.

Who is The Intruders

A trio who believe they are doing holy work by freeing souls: Trish with her axe and skull mask, Baghead with a huge blade, and Zeke, the little one.

How The Intruders plays

Horror 3, but 6 health for each of the three. It is not one killer but three bodies covering the board at once.

Game details from the Final Girl Wiki, under a CC BY-SA licence.

How The Intruders works

  • A trio of killers with 6 health each; they start at Horror 3 in the same space.
  • The Killer Action on their sheet is for "All Killers": each one, from the top down, attacks whoever is in its space. They do not move as part of it.
  • Only the Active Killer resolves Terror Cards.
  • The Active Killer changes when another one is attacked or damaged, through card effects, or when the active one dies.

Summarised from the Final Girl Wiki page (CC BY-SA). Designer clarification on BGG.

Comes in the A Knock at the Door box, paired with Wingard Cottage.
