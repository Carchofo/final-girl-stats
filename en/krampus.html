Across 602 recorded games, the Final Girl beats Krampus 58.3% of the time. The average for the whole game is 65.5%, so that is below average: one of the tough ones.

Who is Krampus

A bipedal creature with huge horns, grey fur and a reptile tongue. According to the lore he was human before his banishment.

How Krampus plays

10 health and Horror 4. His phase chains a lot: he attacks, picks a target again, moves and leaves a present.

Game details from the Final Girl Wiki, under a CC BY-SA licence.

Comes in the The North Pole Nightmare box, paired with Santa's Village.

The map changes the fight

On Santa's Village, its box location, the Final Girl wins 46.7%, against 58.3% across all maps: -11.6 points. The map works for you, not for the killer. The best map is USS Konrad (82.7%) and the worst is Carnival of Blood (37.2%).
