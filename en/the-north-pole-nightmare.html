Krampus on Santa's Village. Across 180 recorded games it is won 46.7% of the time, 18.9 points below the game average (65.5%).

What makes it different

Present system: the elves (the victims) reveal presents with a random good or bad effect, and you can risk opening them. It brings its own vehicle, the reindeer. Along with Shriek, the only box that comes with miniatures. Krampus starts with 10 health and Horror 4, according to a community source.

LevelMedium
The mapA village with the Christmas tree in the middle

How Krampus works

  • Base movement: attacks first and then moves, in its Killer Action.
  • Before the Finale: Target Final Girl or Victim > Attack > Target Final Girl or Victim > Movement > Leave a Present.
  • Leaves facedown Present tokens in or next to his space by die roll and Panic numbers; never two in one space.
  • Presents can be good or bad; Krampus or Santa icons draw from the matching Presents deck.
  • You may open them in the Action phase; a Victim sharing a space with a Present must open it.

The map: Santa's Village

16 spaces and 3 exits (northwest, southwest and southeast). Search spaces: Santa's House, Santa's Workshop and Stables; other named spaces: Runway and Village Center.

  • The Victims are Elves instead.
  • Entering a space with Elves, you lose 1 Time for each Elf present.
