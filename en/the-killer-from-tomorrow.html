Hunter on Sunnydaze Mall. Across 140 recorded games it is won 60.7% of the time, 4.8 points below the game average (65.5%).

What makes it different

Two stages (Mark V → Mark X) with different stats. Damage strips off cybernetic parts before he changes stage. There is a VIP victim, the Savior, who must be protected. Frustrating on its own map because rescuing the Savior depends heavily on the dice. Better on other maps. It is also among the most rules-heavy killers in the game.

How Hunter works

  • Base movement: moves in its Killer Action before attacking.
  • Before the Finale: Target Final Girl > Movement > Attack.
  • First you face the Mark V, tracked by 5 Cybernetic Part tokens; each needs 2 damage in the same phase.
  • Bloodlust only rises when it damages the Final Girl or an ally, or is damaged, at most once per phase.
  • When all parts are gone the Mark V shuts down and its Dark Power goes under the Mark V Ally card; the Mark X follows.

The map: Sunnydaze Mall

22 spaces and 2 exits (southeast and northwest, the Service Entrance). Five are Railing spaces leading to the Food Court. Search spaces: Military Surplus 'R Us, Stan's Sporting Goods and Zap Shack; other Stores: Boards 'N Bones, Joystick Heroes and Smokin' Subject; plus the Atrium and Security.

  • The Savior: the Killer targets him instead of you when resolving Terror cards; if he dies the game is lost immediately. He starts with 4 Health.
  • Trust: raised with the Build Trust card and limits the Savior; only at level 4 can he exit the board and use all Ally actions.
  • Railings: the Final Girl or Savior can jump to the Food Court at the risk of damage; Enemies will not jump.
  • Anyone damaged on a Railing space is sent to the Food Court and loses additional Health.
  • Stores: anyone damaged in a Store is sent through a wall into another space, destroying it (making them adjacent) and taking damage.

Unique cards: Savior Cards (10), Trust, Build Trust
