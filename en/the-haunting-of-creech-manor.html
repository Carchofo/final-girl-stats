Poltergeist on Creech Manor. Across 238 recorded games it is won 42.0% of the time, 23.5 points below the game average (65.5%).

What makes it different

The Poltergeist has NO health and cannot be damaged. You win by finding Carolyn and getting her out. The one-way streets make movement even more awkward.

LevelAdvanced
The mapOne-way streets, few connections

How Poltergeist works

  • Has no Health and cannot be harmed by attacks.
  • To win, find Carolyn in the Item decks and reach an Exit space with her in your inventory.

The map: Creech Manor

Side-on view of a five-story manor with 21 spaces, 3 of them one-way movement spaces. 3 exits, all outside the manor. Search spaces: Attic, Closet and Garage; other named locations: Foyer, Ballroom and Trophy Room.

  • Two of the windows are one-way routes down to the ground floor; the third has a ladder back up, so it is two-way.
  • The top floor has a one-way route to the bathroom below.
