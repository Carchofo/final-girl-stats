Inkanyamba on Sacred Groves. Across 141 recorded games it is won 38.3% of the time, 27.2 points below the game average (65.5%).

What makes it different

The killer's Wrath track stacks with the map's Divine Wrath. Both act at the same time. Consensus: this pairing is brutal. Inkanyamba on another map is far more manageable.

How Inkanyamba works

  • Uses the Killer Wrath card with a marker starting at level 2, ranging from 1 to 10.
  • Before the Finale: if Killer Wrath is 1-2, increase it by 1 > Target Final Girl or Victim > Attack.
  • Unleash Killer Wrath applies the current level's effects, such as raising Horror, a Killer action or extra Health.
  • Atonement Action cards, played in the Action phase, are the main way to reduce Wrath.

The map: Sacred Groves

19 spaces and 3 exits (west, north and southeast). Search spaces: Groundskeeper's Shed, Lost & Found and Welcome Center. Three Sacred Spaces: Burial Ground, Holy Groves and Sacred Shrine.

  • A Divine Wrath tracker rises during the game; when unleashed (via Terror cards or when the Finale is revealed) it applies a detrimental effect based on its level.
  • The location adds effects to the Bloodlust tracker that unleash or increase Divine Wrath.
  • Several effects increase Divine Wrath by the number of Victims at Sacred Spaces.
  • The Atonement Action card reduces Divine Wrath: 3 successes to 1, 2 successes by half (rounded up), 1 success by 1.

Unique cards: Divine Wrath Track, Atonement
