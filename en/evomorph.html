Who is Evomorph

An insectoid alien with a white and red exoskeleton, a scorpion tail and no visible eyes. It was brought on board hidden in the cargo.

How Evomorph plays

It evolves by killing. It starts as a slow but crushing hatchling, becomes a fast and fragile youngling, and ends as a slow, strong adult. Its health rises from 4 up to 10.

Game details from the Final Girl Wiki, under a CC BY-SA licence.

How Evomorph works

  • Base movement: moves in its Killer Action before attacking.
  • Evolves: starts as a Hatchling (10 Damage, 1 Speed, 4 Health, up to 10).
  • As Bloodlust rises it becomes a Youngling (fast, weak), then an Adult (slower, stronger).
  • On reaching Youngling it Vanishes: the next Killer phase is replaced by an Ambush card. At Adult the Dark Power is revealed.
  • Before the Finale: Target Victim > Move > Attack.

Summarised from the Final Girl Wiki page (CC BY-SA).

Comes in the Into the Void box, paired with USS Konrad.
