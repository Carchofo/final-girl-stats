Across 884 recorded games, the Final Girl beats Poltergeist 56.1% of the time. The average for the whole game is 65.5%, so that is below average: one of the tough ones.

Who is Poltergeist

Helen Creech, a spectral woman in a long dress with glowing eyes, roaming her own house.

How Poltergeist plays

The odd one out: she has no health and cannot be attacked. You win by finding Carolyn in the item decks and escaping with her.

Game details from the Final Girl Wiki, under a CC BY-SA licence.

Comes in the The Haunting of Creech Manor box, paired with Creech Manor.

The map changes the fight

On Creech Manor, its box location, the Final Girl wins 42.0%, against 56.1% across all maps: -14.1 points. The map works for you, not for the killer. The best map is Hellscape (78.3%) and the worst is Sacred Groves (41.1%).
