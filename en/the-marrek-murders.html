Tormentor on Marrek Warehouse. Across 164 recorded games it is won 71.3% of the time, 5.8 points above the game average (65.5%).

What makes it different

The Tormentor does not appear on the board until the Finale. Before that there are only death traps to disarm. The community does not find disarming traps before the Finale very rewarding.

LevelMedium
The mapBlocked exits, movement through shelving

The map: Marrek Warehouse

21 spaces (4 of them Rack spaces). 3 exits: Main Entrance, Loading Dock A and Loading Dock B. Search spaces: Manager's Office, Receiving Area and Supply Cage; the only other named space is The Pit.

  • Every Exit starts locked, so no Victims can be saved there until it is unlocked by you or automatically via the Terror deck.
  • Rack spaces are high above the board: Victims and Enemies move there normally, but you need the Climb and/or Leap Action cards.
  • Climb or Leap is gained by discarding Walk; discarding Sprint gains both.
  • Climb moves to an adjacent Rack space; Leap moves directly to another Rack space. Failing Leap forces you to move 1 space and -2 Health.

Unique cards: Climb, Leap
