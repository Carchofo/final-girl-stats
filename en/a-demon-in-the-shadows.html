What makes it different

A two-part win: defeat the possessed nun Ursula and then revive her with Divine Blessings. Malice spaces swap your die for the cursed one. Placing all 14 Malice tokens is an instant loss. The most atmospheric killer of Series 4, and the map is among the best in the game.

How Berith works

  • You must drive Berith out of Ursula's body without killing her permanently, using Divine Blessings to improve her survival.
  • Before the Finale it targets the Final Girl or a Victim and attacks once, without moving.
  • The game usually calls it Ursula.

The map: L'Armes Abbey

18 spaces and 3 exits (northeast, southeast and southwest). Search spaces: Sacristy, Museum and Kitchen. Six spaces are Field Spaces (wheat icon) and one in the northeast is the Altar of the Old Gods.

  • The game starts with 4 Malice tokens: one in the Killer's space and one in each of three adjacent spaces.
  • Various cards add Malice in the Active Killer's space or the closest possible space; if a 15th token would be placed, you lose.
  • In a space with Malice you must replace one regular red die with the black Cursed Die. The Benediction Action card removes a Malice token.
  • Blessed Die: two stars on the 6, one star on the 2, and only one blank face (1).
  • Cursed Die: Partial Success replaces the star on the 5, and a Lose 1 Health symbol is on the 1.

Unique cards: Malice (14), Blessed Die, Cursed Die, Tokens (18)
