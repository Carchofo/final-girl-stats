Who is Billy

A tall, wrecked animatronic bear with no casing on his left arm.

How Billy plays

He brings minions that appear during the game and act before him. He also heals 1 every phase while a power source is active, so cutting the power is part of the plan.

How Billy works

  • Base movement: does not move in its Killer Action; attacks whoever is in its space.
  • Has Minions that can spawn during the game and act before him.
  • Before the Finale, after Minions: +1 Health if a power source is active > Target Victim/Final Girl > Strike once.
