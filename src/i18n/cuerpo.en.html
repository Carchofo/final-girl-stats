Who is behind the data

The 13.920 games don’t come from nowhere: people log them, one by one. They are , from those with one game to those with almost a thousand. Look yourself up, and if you’re not there, you know what’s missing.

Why it matters That is 669 people, but twenty-five of them make up 50% of the data, and the top contributor alone 7,3%. 33% have logged a single game. Those who log a lot win more than average, but only a little: 70,1% for the top ten against 65,0% for the rest. That is why comparisons between boxes hold up while absolute values should be taken with a pinch of salt.
