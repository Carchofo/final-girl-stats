Berith auf L'Armes Abbey. In 74 erfassten Partien wird in 48,6 % der Fälle gewonnen, 16,6 Punkte unter dem Spielschnitt (65,3 %).

Kleine Stichprobe

Nur 74 erfasste Partien. Aufgeteilt auf Dark Powers, Finales und Setups bleiben je Auswertung sehr wenige: eher ein Hinweis als ein Fakt.

Was sie besonders macht

Ein zweiteiliger Sieg: Besiege die besessene Nonne Ursula und belebe sie dann mit Divine Blessings wieder. Malice-Felder tauschen deinen Würfel gegen den verfluchten. Alle 14 Malice-Marker zu platzieren bedeutet sofortige Niederlage. Der stimmungsvollste Killer der Serie 4, und die Karte gehört zu den besten des Spiels.

So funktioniert Berith

  • Du musst Berith aus Ursulas Körper vertreiben, ohne sie endgültig zu töten; Divine Blessings verbessern ihre Überlebenschance.
  • Vor dem Finale wählt er das Final Girl oder ein Victim und greift einmal an, ohne sich zu bewegen.
  • Das Spiel nennt ihn meist Ursula.

Die Karte: L'Armes Abbey

18 Felder und 3 Ausgänge (Nordosten, Südosten und Südwesten). Suchfelder: Sacristy, Museum und Kitchen. Sechs Felder sind Field Spaces (Weizensymbol), eines im Nordosten ist der Altar of the Old Gods.

  • Das Spiel beginnt mit 4 Malice-Markern: einer im Feld des Killers und je einer in drei benachbarten Feldern.
  • Verschiedene Karten legen Malice ins Feld des aktiven Killers oder das nächstmögliche Feld; müsste ein 15. Marker gelegt werden, verlierst du.
  • In einem Feld mit Malice musst du einen normalen roten Würfel durch den schwarzen Cursed Die ersetzen. Die Aktionskarte Benediction entfernt einen Malice-Marker.
  • Blessed Die: zwei Sterne auf der 6, ein Stern auf der 2 und nur eine leere Seite (1).
  • Cursed Die: Partial Success ersetzt den Stern auf der 5, auf der 1 steht Lose 1 Health.

Eigene Karten: Malice (14), Blessed Die, Cursed Die, Tokens (18)

Wie man die Partie beginnt

Die vier Eröffnungen, und welche je nach Aufbau passt, stehen in den Eröffnungsstrategien.

📖 Berith guide (killer)

Works with any location. ↑ top

⌛ = Time · ✴ = damage · 🎭 = raise Horror · 🔪 = knife icon (attack) · ⊘🎭 = lower Horror · ❤️ = recover health · 🙏 = blessing icon · 🎲★ = swap 1 normal die for the Blessed die (more success faces) · 🎲☠ = swap 1 normal die for the Cursed die

Watch out

  • Killing Ursula is not a win: you then reveal the blessings and need at least one to show health. With no blessings on the sheet, nothing can bring her back.

Special setup

  • Place the Divine Blessings sheet next to the play area and pick a side: Heavenly (normal) or Hellish (hard).
  • Place the Expel Demon Action cards in the Action Tableau.

Ursula's possession

  • Berith is a demon possessing Ursula, a nun. The Killer meeple, health, Killer Attack and Movement Values are all possessed Ursula's.
  • Once you "kill" Ursula, Berith leaves her body.

Divine Blessings

  • When you see the 🙏 icon, place a black Final Health token face down on the topmost empty space of the Divine Blessings sheet and resolve its effect. You'll mostly get this from the Expel Demon Action card.
  • These blessings are what can bring Ursula back to life once Berith leaves her body.
  • Final Health tokens on the sheet can't be removed or revealed unless something says so.
  • Final Health tokens are limited. If some leave the game through other effects, or are assigned to the Killer or the Final Girl, fewer will be left for the sheet.

Winning the game

  • First kill possessed Ursula with the normal rules, revealing her black Final Health token and restoring her health if needed. Once she's defeated, Berith leaves her body.
  • Then reveal each Final Health token on the Divine Blessings sheet. If any shows health, Ursula comes back and you win. If all are blank, her soul is damned and you lose.
  • You don't need all 7 spaces filled before defeating her, but the more blessings, the likelier she comes back.

📖 L'Armes Abbey guide (location)

Works with any killer. ↑ top

Watch out

  • If all 14 Malice tokens are on the board and you need to place another, you lose immediately.
  • You never roll the Blessed and Cursed dice together: if both icons show up, use the one with more; if they're equal, they cancel out.

Special setup

  • Place a Malice token in the Killer's starting space and in 3 spaces adjacent to the Killer. If there are only 2 adjacent spaces, place one in the next closest space of your choice. You start with 4 Malice tokens.
  • Place the Donkey token in the Cloister space.
  • Place the Benediction Action cards in the Action Tableau.

Blessed and Cursed dice

  • The box has two special dice: the Blessed die (🎲★, more success faces) and the Cursed die (🎲☠). They sometimes replace normal dice in Horror Rolls.
  • When you see one of their icons, replace 1 normal die with that die when making the Horror Roll.
  • If both icons are present but there are more of one kind, replace 1 die with that kind. If there's an equal number of each, they cancel out and neither die is used.
  • If your final Horror Roll result shows the Cursed die's bad icon, you lose health (💔).
  • The effects of the Improvise Action card also apply to the 5 on the Cursed die.

Malice

  • When you see the Malice icon on a card, place a Malice token in the Active Killer's space. If it already has one, place it in the closest space to the Killer without a token.
  • With multiple Active Killers, only the one closest to you counts.
  • If there's no Killer on the board, reveal a random Setup card and treat its K space as the Killer's space.
  • Malice tokens carry the Cursed die symbol: when you make a Horror Roll in a space with Malice, replace 1 normal die with the Cursed die.
  • Malice tokens removed (by the Benediction Action card or other effects) go back to the general pool.

What the cards tell us

  • The saints' relics give 🎲★ when resolving certain cards, meaning you swap a normal die for the Blessed die.
  • The Padlock Key lets the Donkey move with you.
  • Relics and events lean hard on the Blessed die and Malice tokens: see below.

Items

Holy Water

1 hand · 3 uses. 🎲★ for a Horror Roll or remove a Malice token in your space. If you have the Aspergillum in your other hand, also remove a Malice token in an adjacent space. There are 2 in the box.

Holy Chalice

Discard during the Action phase for 3 ❤️ or discard when your black Final Health token is revealed: if it's blank, you come back with 1 ❤️ instead.

Rosary

There are 2 in the box. 2 uses. Spend 2 ⌛ during the Killer phase to ignore a 🎭.

Aspergillum

Weapon · range 0 · 1 ✴ · 1 hand. If you have Holy Water in your other hand, you may spend one of its uses to deal 1 extra ✴.

Thurible

Weapon · range 0-1 · 1 ✴ · 1 hand. If used at range 1, discard it.

Crucifix

1 hand. Discard to ignore a Horror level increase or to end an Enemy's movement when they're in an adjacent space.

St. Peter's Sword

Weapon · range 0 · 2 ✴ · 2 hands. 🎲★ when resolving Action cards that perform attacks. The Sword can't be used to modify Reaction cards.

St. Longinus' Spear

Weapon · range 0 · 1 ✴ · 1 hand. 🎲★ when resolving Action cards that perform attacks. If you damage an Enemy with the Spear, you may move them 1 space.

St. Lucy's Eyes

🎲★ when resolving Action cards that let you take Items. Draw and keep 1 extra Item.

Padlock Key

The Donkey may move with you. When moving with it, roll a die: 1-3 move 1 less space (min 1), 4-6 move 1 extra. Once per Action phase, in a space with the Donkey and an Enemy, roll a die: on 5-6 the Enemy takes 2 ✴.

L'Armes Abbey events

Sins of the Past
Roll 3 dice. If you get what the card shows on all 3 (three red icons), you are the Devil's child and immediately lose. On any other result, place the Repentance token in the Sanctuary: while it's on the board, ignore all 🎲★ effects. Remove it by going to the Sanctuary and spending 3 ⌛.
Leap of Faith
Look through the unused Item cards and take 1.
Pilgrimage
Place 2 Victims in the Hospitium, 2 in the Refectory and 2 in the Sanctuary.
Black Mass
During the Upkeep phase, place a Malice token if no Malice tokens were added this turn.
Maddening Nursery Rhyme
In the space with the most Victims, a Victim is killed and every other Victim there panics.
Breaking the Curse
Take a Benediction Action card from the Action Tableau. If both are in your hand, ⊘🎭.
Father Damian
Place Father Damian in the Sacristy. He follows you into the Killer's space and can only be attacked if there are no other Victims in his space. While he's in your space, 🎲★ for all Horror Rolls and, once per Action phase, you may remove a Malice token in your space. If he's killed in your space, 🎭🎭🎭.
Demonic Crocodile
Place the Crocodile token in the Museum. Enemies can't target or kill it, but you may attack and kill it. Movement, Attack and health of 1. At the end of the Killer phase it targets the Final Girl, moves and attacks. Then all Victims in its space panic.
The Quebecer
Place him in the Dormitory. Normal Victims in his space won't follow you unless he comes too. With him, an extra Victim may follow you. If he's in your space during Upkeep, ⌛ per Victim in your space.
The Abbess
Place her in the Abbess' Office. During Upkeep she moves one space towards the closest normal Victim not in the Dormitory. Normal Victims in her starting and ending space move 2 spaces towards the Dormitory.
